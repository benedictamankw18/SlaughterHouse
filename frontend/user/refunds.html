Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Contact us about an order

For a cancellation or refund request, contact us as soon as possible at derryakyene99@gmail.com or sallywaves28@gmail.com. Include your order or payment reference, the email used at checkout, and a short description of the issue. Do not send card details, passwords, or one-time security codes.

We review requests based on the order, whether a digital product or service has been delivered or used, payment status, and applicable consumer-protection requirements. A refund is not automatic merely because a request is submitted. This policy does not limit rights that applicable law gives you.

Bookings

A booking payment submits a request; it does not by itself guarantee the requested slot. If we cannot provide a confirmed session, the session payment will be refunded. Contact us promptly if you need to change or cancel a booking. We will review the request and confirm available options; a change or refund cannot be guaranteed once a session has been confirmed or delivered.

If a payment is received after a reservation expires or a requested slot is no longer available, we may contact you to arrange an alternative session or review a refund.

Beat licenses and digital products

Contact us promptly if a digital purchase was duplicated, was not authorized by you, or you cannot access the purchased files. We will investigate the order and payment. If a full refund is issued, the associated license may be revoked and download access may be disabled. A partial refund may have different effects under the specific license terms.

Refund decisions remain subject to applicable law and the circumstances of the order. The specific license rights saved with a purchase are described in the Beat License Terms.

Merchandise

If merchandise is missing, damaged, incorrect, or cannot be fulfilled, contact us with your order reference and relevant details so we can review the issue. Refund or replacement options depend on the circumstances and applicable law. Please contact us before returning anything so we can provide instructions.

Payment processing

Approved refunds are submitted through Paystack where applicable. The time it takes for funds to appear depends on the payment method and the financial institution. We cannot control their processing times. We will use the original payment method where the provider supports it.
