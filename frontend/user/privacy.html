Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Information we collect

When you use the store, we may collect information you provide, including your name, email address, phone number, account details, artist or project information, delivery details, reviews, and messages or files submitted with a request. When you place an order or request a booking, we keep the order details, selected items or session slots, payment status, and related support records.

Paystack processes payment details for us. We receive payment references, status, amount, and currency needed to confirm and reconcile an order. We do not ask you to send card numbers or payment passwords to us by email or message.

Our systems also process technical and security data such as IP address, request identifiers, session and CSRF tokens, and event timestamps to operate the service, prevent abuse, and investigate errors.

How we use information

  • Create and protect customer or administrator accounts and verify email addresses.
  • Process payments, deliver purchases, manage licenses, bookings, refunds, and customer support.
  • Send service messages about accounts, orders, bookings, and payment outcomes.
  • Operate the website, maintain records, prevent fraud or misuse, and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use the website to send promotional email unless we separately obtain the required permission.

Service providers and sharing

We share only the information needed with providers that support the service. These may include Paystack for payments, the configured hosting and database providers, an S3-compatible storage provider for media, and the configured email delivery provider. Their processing is subject to their own terms and privacy notices. Some pages load fonts or currency flag images from Google Fonts or FlagCDN; those providers may receive technical connection data when your browser requests those resources.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a business transfer. We do not publish customer payment credentials. Public license verification pages show only the privacy-safe details needed to verify a certificate.

Storage, retention, and your choices

We retain account, order, payment, license, and support records for as long as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, maintain financial records, and meet legal obligations. Retention periods may differ by record type. Information in submitted files may be retained as part of the relevant request, order, or license.

You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, subject to identity checks and records we must retain. Deleting browser storage can remove locally saved cart and session state but does not delete server-side account or order records.

Security and contact

We use safeguards such as protected sessions, access controls, and payment-provider verification. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

For privacy questions or requests, contact derryakyene99@gmail.com or sallywaves28@gmail.com.

We may update this policy as the service changes. The date above indicates the latest revision.
