Cookie Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Cookies used for account sessions

When you sign in, the service may set an administrator or customer session cookie. These cookies are used to recognize your authenticated session. They are marked HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax; in production they are also marked Secure. The server controls their validity and expiration.

These cookies are necessary for account and administrative features. If you block them, sign-in or protected account actions may not work correctly.

Browser storage

The website also uses session storage and local storage, which are browser storage mechanisms rather than cookies. They may hold temporary sign-in state, CSRF values, cart and pending-payment state, selected account details, audio preview settings, and playback position. This supports the features you choose to use.

You can clear cookies and browser storage through your browser settings. This may sign you out, empty your saved cart, or reset local preferences. Clearing browser data does not delete orders or account records held by the service.

Third-party resources

Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts and currency flag images from FlagCDN. If your browser requests those resources, the providers may receive technical information such as your IP address. Payment checkout and processing are provided by Paystack and may use their own cookies or similar technologies under Paystack's policies.

We do not intentionally use advertising cookies or analytics trackers on the storefront. If this changes, we will update this policy and provide any notices or choices required by law.
