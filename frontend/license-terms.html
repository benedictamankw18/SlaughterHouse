Your purchase record controls

Each certificate records a snapshot of the license rights and restrictions saved when the beat was purchased. The certificate and its verification record identify that purchase; they do not replace the purchase terms or expand the rights granted. If a certificate is revoked, refunded, or reissued, its public verification status reflects that change.

Always refer to the specific rights listed on your certificate. License definitions may change for future purchases, but changes do not rewrite the saved rights for an earlier purchase.

New beat licenses purchased under these terms are valid for one year from the purchase date. Licenses purchased under earlier terms keep their original duration; an earlier license with no recorded expiry does not expire. Downloads and license permissions require a fully paid order and an unexpired, unrevoked license. Partial refunds suspend access. To renew an expired license, purchase the license again.

How recorded rights are described

  • File formats: identifies the delivered audio formats recorded for the purchase.
  • Commercial use: indicates whether commercial usage is included in the saved license snapshot.
  • Distribution: indicates whether distribution rights are included. Any listed distribution conditions also apply.
  • Usage limits: states recorded limits for the specified usage category. “Unlimited” means no numeric limit was saved for that category.
  • Resale: any resale prohibition applies as stated in the saved purchase terms.

Ownership and verification

A beat license grants only the permissions recorded in the associated purchase terms. It does not by itself transfer copyright ownership. The QR code and public verification link let a recipient confirm certificate identity and current status; the public record intentionally shows only a privacy-safe customer name.

For help with a purchase, contact UltBigBoy Music Production and include the certificate ID or order reference.
